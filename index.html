Editor’s Note

Outside India, the name Meghnad Saha (1893–1956) may be unfamiliar, yet his scientific legacy is woven into the fabric of modern astrophysics. His celebrated Saha ionization equation transformed our understanding of stellar atmospheres, showing how a star’s spectrum encodes its temperature and chemical composition — one of the foundational insights of twentieth-century astrophysics. Yet Saha’s life tells a far larger story than a single scientific breakthrough.
